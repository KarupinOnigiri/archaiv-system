Getting Started

Signing in. Use your username (or barcode ID) and password. Admin accounts with an RFID card set up can also just tap the card against a reader — the login page detects it automatically and switches to a PIN pad, no button to press first.

Your role. What you see in the sidebar and what buttons are available depends on your role: Admin (full access), In Charge (day-to-day operations plus most settings), and Tool Keeper (checkout/return and inventory viewing, plus filing unrestriction requests instead of acting directly). If a button or tab you'd expect isn't visible, it's almost always because your role doesn't have it — ask an admin rather than assuming something's broken.

Locked out of a tab? An admin can turn Inventory, Members, Borrow & Return, Approvals, or Reports off app-wide from System → Feature Access. If a tab you normally use has vanished for everyone, that's likely why.

Keyboard shortcuts. Press ? from anywhere to see every shortcut for the page you're on — searchable, and grouped by page. Alt+Shift+ plus a letter jumps straight to any sidebar tab from wherever you are (hover a tab while the sidebar's expanded to see its letter). Shortcuts are disabled while you're typing in a field, and while a dialog is open.

Dashboard

Your landing page — a one-screen overview of what's happening right now: total/available assets, active and overdue loans, a recent-activity feed, top-borrowed items, and your own notifications, all in one glance with no scrolling.

Quick Actions lets you jump straight into a checkout, a return, or (for Admin/In Charge) adding a new member or asset, without navigating to that tab first.

Inventory

The full list of physical assets — tools, equipment, instruments. Search, filter by status/condition/category, and sort any column by clicking its header.

Scanning. Click the search box and scan a barcode (or type it and hit Enter) to jump straight to that item's details, from anywhere on the page.

Adding/editing {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}(Add New Item / Edit) lets you set the name, category, RFID tag, condition, and up to three photos.{% else %}is available to Admin and In Charge accounts.{% endif %}

Deleting {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}archives an item rather than erasing it — it moves to the Archived view (toggle it with the archive icon in the toolbar) and can be restored later.{% else %}archives an item; only Admin and In Charge can do this.{% endif %} {% if staff.role == "admin" %}Restoring an archived item is admin-only.{% endif %}

Auto-delete. An admin can set a retention window (System → Auto-Delete Archived Items) after which archived items are permanently removed on their own — off by default, so nothing disappears unless an admin turns it on.

Bulk actions — select multiple rows with the checkboxes to delete or (for archived items) restore several at once.

Import/Export CSV {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}bulk-adds or downloads inventory from a spreadsheet — Import previews what will be created before you confirm, and a completed import can be undone (re-archives everything it just created) right from the results screen.{% else %}is available to Admin and In Charge accounts.{% endif %}

Members

Everyone who can borrow tools — trainees and trainers. Same search/filter/sort/scan conventions as Inventory.

Restrictions. A member's account can be flagged restricted (blocks new checkouts) either automatically — after too many items are returned damaged, see Damage Restriction in System — or manually{% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %} by an Admin/In Charge account{% endif %}.

Lifting a restriction. {% if staff.role == "tool_keeper" %}As a Tool Keeper, you can't lift a restriction directly — file an unrestriction request instead (from the member's row), and an Admin or In Charge account reviews it from the Approvals tab.{% else %}Admin/In Charge can lift a restriction directly, or review a Tool Keeper's unrestriction request from the Approvals tab.{% endif %}

Deactivating {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}removes a member from active borrowing (they move to the Inactive view) without deleting their history — reactivate any time. If that member also has a staff account, it's deactivated along with them.{% else %}is available to Admin and In Charge accounts.{% endif %}

Import/Export CSV {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}works the same way as Inventory's — bulk-add a whole batch or department at once from a spreadsheet.{% else %}is available to Admin and In Charge accounts.{% endif %}

Borrow & Return

New Checkout walks you through picking a borrower, then adding assets by scanning or searching, and confirms with a due date (defaults to the app-wide Loan Duration setting, but you can change it per checkout).

Return Items scans or searches for the loan, lets you record the returned condition, and note any remarks. Marking anything other than Functional automatically puts that asset into Maintenance; marking it Disposed removes it from circulation.

Batch return handles multiple items borrowed by the same person in one go, applying the same condition/remarks to all of them.

Extending a due date ("Renew") is available from a loan's row for anything still borrowed.

Overdue loans are swept automatically every 15 minutes — a member with an overdue item gets auto-restricted without anyone needing to notice and act on it manually.

{% endif %} {% if feature_flags.requests %}

Approvals

Unrestriction requests — filed by a Tool Keeper on a restricted member's behalf, reviewed (approved/denied) by Admin or In Charge.

Admin deactivation requests — deactivating an admin account isn't a single click, on purpose. Any active In Charge or Tool Keeper account can vote to approve or deny it (every in_charge/tool_keeper is notified the moment one's filed). Normally that needs 4 approvals with at least 2 from In Charge accounts; if there aren't enough In Charge/Tool Keeper staff around for that, it scales down to 3 approvals (1+ In Charge) or 2 (1+ In Charge) depending on how many actually exist. Votes don't do anything by themselves, though — reaching the required count doesn't auto-deactivate anyone, and a single denial doesn't cancel the request either. The admin who filed it has the final say: they can open "View reviews" to see every vote and its reason, then either Finalize (once enough approvals are in) or Cancel at any time regardless of vote counts.

Each tab remembers its own filter/status and page position in the URL, so a bookmarked or refreshed link reopens exactly where you left it.

{% endif %}

Notifications

Alerts land here for loans you're tracking nearing their due date ("due soon"), loans that have gone overdue, and (if enabled) unrestriction request updates. The bell icon in the header shows your unread count and a quick-view dropdown.

What counts as "due soon" is an app-wide threshold {% if staff.role in ["admin", "in_charge"] %}an admin sets from System → Notification Settings{% else %}an admin controls{% endif %} — how many hours before the due date the alert fires.

Which alerts reach you personally is controlled per-account from the account menu's "Settings & privacy" — due-soon reminders, overdue alerts, and unrestriction-request updates can each be turned on or off independently, without affecting anyone else.

{% if staff.role == "admin" %}

Staff

Admin-only. Manage every staff account: create new ones (grants system access to an existing member), edit role/assignment window/RFID card, lock/unlock, reset a forgotten password, deactivate/reactivate.

Roles — Admin, In Charge, Tool Keeper — are set here. A Tool Keeper account needs both an assigned start and end date to be able to sign in at all; the other two roles don't require it. Reassign Usage (on a Tool Keeper's row) is the fast way to set that window — the quick-extend buttons always count from the start date, filling it in with today's date first if it's blank, and Clear wipes both dates back out.

Handing out admin access is a direct action here (unlike removing it, which routes through peer approval on the Approvals tab) — creating a new account with the Admin role, or promoting an existing one, takes effect immediately.

RFID card + PIN for card-tap login are assigned here too — a card alone isn't enough to sign in, it always needs its paired 6-digit PIN.

Import/Export CSV bulk-creates staff accounts for members who already exist as borrowers.

{% endif %} {% if feature_flags.reports %}

Reports

Charts and tables built from your actual data — most/least-borrowed assets, borrowed-by-category, loan duration trends, top borrowers, overdue frequency, condition changes, and staff activity.{% if staff.role == "tool_keeper" %} As a Tool Keeper you see a smaller set, scoped only to your own activity — never other staff's or an all-borrowers view.{% endif %}

Date range applies to every widget at once — pick a preset or a custom range at the top of the page.

Customize lets you show/hide and reorder individual widgets. Export downloads the current view.

Activity Log

A running audit trail of every meaningful action taken in the app — who did what, to what, and when. Filter by staff member, action type, or date range. Useful for tracing back "who deleted this" or "when did this get approved."

Account & Security

Everything below lives in the account menu (click your name/avatar in the sidebar):

  • My Account — your profile photo, name, and (if you're an admin) RFID card PIN.
  • Change password — you'll stay signed in on this device; any other active sessions on your account are signed out.
    • {% if staff.role == "admin" %}
  • Change RFID PIN — admin-only, pairs with your RFID card for tap-to-login. Requires your current password to confirm, not your current PIN.
    • {% endif %}
  • Display & accessibility — Theme mode (Mixed/Light/Dark/Modern Dark) and Notification style (Light/Dark/Material Design), both picked from a dropdown and applied immediately, saved to this account on this browser.
  • Settings & privacy — which alert types land in your own notification bell.
  • Give feedback — send a bug report or idea straight to admins, with optional screenshots, without leaving the app.
  • Log out — asks you to confirm by name before signing out.
