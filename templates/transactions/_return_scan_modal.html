{# Scan-driven return, mirroring the checkout modal's shape (identify -> pick items -> confirm) but deliberately lighter: no server-side session row, no minimize/dock, no resume-across-reload. A return interaction is short (a handful of items, confirm, done) rather than something staff get interrupted mid-way through the way a checkout can be, so all state just lives in JS for the lifetime of this modal and is thrown away if it's closed without confirming. Step 2 shows EVERY open loan for the member the moment their barcode is entered -- not just whatever gets scanned -- so staff can see the full expected list and notice if something's missing, and so a worn/unreadable barcode isn't a dead end (the row can just be clicked). Scanning is still there as the fast path: it's validated through the same _find_open_transaction_for_scan() the single-scan kiosk route uses, and on success just checks the matching row instead of adding a separate entry -- there's one list, not a checklist plus a separate cart. #}
