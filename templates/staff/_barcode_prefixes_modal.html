Barcode ID Prefixes

The letters at the start of every generated Asset ID, Member Code, and Staff Code (e.g. "AST00001"). Changing one only affects codes generated from now on - existing records keep whatever prefix they already have.

{{ asset_id_prefix }}00001
{{ member_code_prefix }}00001
{{ staff_code_prefix }}00001

2-6 characters: letters, numbers, or - _ . # (no spaces).
