App-wide defaults for preventive maintenance scheduling on serialized assets (Inventory → an asset's Edit form/Details panel).

When a serialized asset's Next Maintenance Due is left blank, it's auto-set to this many days out from its last calibration date (or acquisition date, or today if neither is on record) instead of staying unscheduled.

How many days ahead of an asset's due date (or how close to its usage-interval trigger) counts as "due soon" - drives the Dashboard's Maintenance Due card and the maintenance-due notification everyone gets in their bell.
